Positive backscatter change indicates an increase in radar backscatter response. Negative change indicates a decrease. These changes may be related to changes in surface properties but are not direct evidence of urban construction or demolition.

The joint candidate masks identify pixels in which both VV and VH changes exceed their respective period-specific 95th or 5th percentile thresholds. They are exploratory SAR change candidates, not validated urban expansion classes.
