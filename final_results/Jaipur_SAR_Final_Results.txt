INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The detected SAR clusters represent exploratory backscatter-change candidates.
They are not independently verified urban expansion or construction labels.
Current OpenStreetMap data were used as spatial reference information.
Current OSM building footprints do not establish the historical presence or absence of buildings in 2016 or 2020.
The percentage of SAR candidate area overlapping current OSM buildings is a spatial overlap statistic, not a classification accuracy score.
Further historical imagery interpretation and independent validation are required before claiming quantified urban expansion.
